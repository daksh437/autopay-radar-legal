Delete your data

Last updated: 20 August 2026

AutoPay Radar keeps your financial data — the transactions, autopays, balances, merchants and account numbers read from your bank messages — only on your phone. There is no account and no server, so nobody but you ever holds it.

The short version: everything that matters is deleted the moment you wipe it inside the app. The only data that ever leaves your phone is anonymous crash and usage information, which carries nothing about your money and can be deleted on request.

1. Delete everything on your phone (instant)

All of your financial data lives on the device. To erase it completely:

  1. Open AutoPay Radar.
  2. Go to Settings (the slider icon, top right of the Home screen).
  3. Under Your data, tap Delete everything.
  4. Confirm. Every transaction and autopay is removed from the phone at once.

Uninstalling the app also removes this data. None of it was ever copied anywhere else.

2. Delete the anonymous crash & usage data

The app sends anonymous crash reports and usage counts (via Google Firebase) to help fix bugs. This data contains no message content, no amounts, no merchant or account details. To have it deleted, email instaflow38@gmail.com from the address you wish to reference, with the subject “Delete my data”. We action such requests within 30 days.

What is deleted, and what is kept

  • Deleted: all on-device data (transactions, autopays, balances, merchants, account tails) — immediately, by you, from inside the app.
  • Deleted on request: anonymous crash and usage records held by our analytics provider.
  • Automatically removed: crash logs are retained for at most 90 days before automatic deletion.
  • Kept: nothing that identifies you or your finances — because none of it ever leaves your device.
