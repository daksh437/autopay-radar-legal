AutoPay Radar reads bank and payment messages on your phone to find recurring payments and warn you before they are charged. That reading happens entirely on your device. Your messages, transactions and account details are never sent to us or to anyone else.

The short version

  • We have no account system and no server that stores your financial data.
  • SMS content is read on your phone, turned into transactions there, and stored there.
  • We never transmit, sell, or share your messages, amounts, merchants or account numbers.
  • Crash reports and anonymous usage counts do leave your device. They contain no financial data.
  • You can delete everything from inside the app at any time.

2. What the app reads, and why

SMS messages

The app requests READ_SMS and RECEIVE_SMS. This is the core functionality of the app: without it there is nothing to detect. The app scans your SMS inbox and incoming messages to identify bank and payment alerts, and extracts the amount, date, merchant, bank, last digits of the account, and any reference number.

The app deliberately ignores messages that are not financial: one-time passwords, promotional offers, and marketing. All of this processing happens on your device. SMS content is never uploaded, transmitted, or shared.

Notification access (optional)

If you turn on notification access, the app reads notifications from a fixed list of banking and payment applications only, to catch payments that arrive as app notifications rather than SMS, and to power the Soundbox feature. Notifications from any other app are ignored. This is optional and the app works without it.

4. What we never do

  • We never ask for your UPI PIN, card CVV, net-banking password, or any OTP. No screen in the app requests them.
  • We never move money, initiate a payment, or cancel a mandate on your behalf.
  • We never use your financial data for advertising or marketing, and we do not show ads.
  • We never sell or rent data to anyone.

6. Where your data is stored

In a database inside the app's private storage on your phone. It is not backed up to us. If you use the in-app backup, a file is created and you choose where it goes — we never receive it.

7. How long we keep it

Financial data stays on your phone until you delete it. Uninstalling the app removes it. Crash and usage records held by Google are retained per Firebase's published retention periods.

8. Your controls

  • Delete everything — Settings → Delete everything wipes all stored transactions and mandates.
  • Export — take your data out as CSV, PDF or a backup file.
  • Revoke permissions — Android Settings → Apps → AutoPay Radar → Permissions. The app keeps working with reduced ability.
  • Uninstall — removes all local data.

9. Children

The app is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

10. Security

Data stays in the app's private storage, which Android isolates from other apps. Crash and usage data is encrypted in transit. No system is perfectly secure, and a rooted or compromised device weakens these protections.
